A verification step for after-sales claims. It reads the set of documents behind a claim and checks them as a set, not one at a time.

## What it does

- **Completeness**: confirms each document carries what makes it valid, such as a stamp, a signature and a duty stamp.
- **Cross-document consistency**: compares the details that should match across the documents of one claim.
- **Tax fields**: checks the tax figures a compliant invoice must carry.
- **Fraud signals**: flags documents that look altered or reused.

## Approach

Most problems are not visible in a single document; they show up when two documents disagree. Reading every document into structured data first is what makes that comparison possible, and the result is a list of specific findings for a reviewer, not a bare pass or fail.
