A validator for vehicle registration (STNK) and ownership (BPKB) documents uploaded by the network.

## What it does

- **Completeness**: confirms the required documents are present.
- **Image quality**: checks that a photo is clear, readable and not cropped.
- **Authenticity**: checks whether the registration photo is genuine.
- **Monitoring**: a report by period lists documents that are not yet acceptable or not yet finished.

## Approach

Checking at upload moves the problem to the moment it can still be fixed cheaply, when the person with the document is still there, instead of weeks later during review.
