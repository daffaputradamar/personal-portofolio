**Legal Talk** answers the routine questions people have before they submit something to the legal team: what is required, what the procedure is, and what a correct document looks like.

## What it does

- **Answers from the source**: replies come from the legal team's own guidance, retrieved for each question.
- **Requirements up front**: the assistant lists what a request needs and provides example documents.
- **Fewer round trips**: people learn the requirements before submitting, instead of after a rejection.

## Approach

The assistant covers guidance, not judgement. It explains procedure and requirements from approved material and leaves decisions on an actual case to the legal team.
