A helpdesk assistant with one job done well: answering "what is happening with my ticket?".

## What it does

- **Ticket lookup**: checks the progress of a ticket in the service desk system and reports it in plain language.
- **Self-service**: the employee gets the answer without waiting for an agent.
- **Usage view**: a dashboard shows how much of the question load the assistant absorbs.

## Approach

The scope was chosen from the helpdesk's own conversation data. Status questions were frequent, simple and fully answerable from the ticketing system, which makes them the right first thing to automate.
