A reader for Indonesian family cards. It turns a photographed card into structured fields that can go straight into the dealer system, instead of being retyped.

## What it does

- **Reading**: extracts the card's fields and the members listed on it.
- **Mapping**: places each value in the right field, which is where earlier reading most often went wrong.
- **Irregular cards**: handles the older and non-standard layouts as well as the regular one.
- **Validation**: checks the card number and identity numbers against existing customer data.

## Approach

Reading and validation are separate steps. A value can be read correctly and still be wrong for the customer, so the checks against existing records run on the structured result, not on the image.
