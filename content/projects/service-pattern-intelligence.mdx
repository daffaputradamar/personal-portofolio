A recommendation system for after-sales service. It looks at how a particular vehicle has actually been used and serviced, and suggests what it needs next.

## What it does

- **Per-vehicle recommendations**: suggestions follow the vehicle's own history, not only the standard interval.
- **Pattern analysis**: finds service patterns across the historical data.
- **Advisor support**: gives the service advisor a data-backed starting point for the conversation.

## Approach

A fixed schedule over-services some vehicles and under-services others. Scheduled jobs build the recommendations from historical data, so they are ready when the vehicle arrives and do not depend on anyone running an analysis at the counter.
