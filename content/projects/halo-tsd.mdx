**Halo TSD** is a support assistant for the service network. It answers day-to-day operational and technical questions from a knowledge base the support team maintains, instead of from whoever happens to be online.

## What it does

- **Grounded answers**: replies are drawn from the team's guides and manuals, retrieved at question time.
- **Sign-in**: only network users can ask, so the knowledge base is not exposed publicly.
- **Escalation**: when no document answers the question, the assistant says so and points to the person responsible for that topic.
- **Owned by the experts**: the support team uploads and updates the documents and watches answer quality.

## Approach

The assistant is deliberately narrow. It answers from the registered documents and nothing else, and a missing answer becomes a hand-off rather than a guess, which is what makes it safe to put in front of people doing operational work.
