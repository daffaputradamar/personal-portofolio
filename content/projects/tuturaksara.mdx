**Tuturaksara** converts recorded audio into text, and then into a finished document, most often the minutes of a meeting.

## What it does

- **Transcription**: audio in, timestamped text out.
- **Templated output**: the transcript is rendered into a document structure such as minutes of meeting, rather than handed over as a raw wall of text.
- **Feeds downstream tools**: its transcripts are one of the inputs to [Rewangit](/projects/rewangit), which covers the wider document-generation case.

## Approach

Transcription and document assembly are separate stages. Keeping them apart means the transcript remains a verifiable record of what was actually said, while the generated minutes are a derived artefact that can be regenerated or reformatted without re-running the audio.
