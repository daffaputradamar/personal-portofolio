**OPER** (On-Point Event Recommendation) helps sales teams decide where an event is most likely to pay off.

## What it does

- **Recommendations with reasons**: each suggested location comes with the indicators behind it, so the choice can be questioned.
- **Map view**: recommendations and past events are shown geographically.
- **Performance dashboard**: follows an event from its funnel and productivity to the units sold.
- **Alerts**: tells the area team when a recommended location has not had an event.

## Approach

The data warehouse is the single source, so a recommendation and the report that later judges it are built from the same numbers. Showing the reasoning matters as much as the recommendation; a location nobody can explain is one nobody will act on.
