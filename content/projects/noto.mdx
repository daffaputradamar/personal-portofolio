**NOTO** takes minutes for the sales team. It records the conversation during a visit, review or meeting and turns it into minutes people can act on.

## What it does

- **Recording on a phone**: captures audio during the visit, from a mobile device.
- **Draft minutes**: the transcript is shaped into structured minutes, not handed over as raw text.
- **Review and approval**: the sales team edits and approves the draft before it becomes final.
- **Distribution**: the approved minutes go to the people who attended.
- **Follow-up**: open problems and to-dos are tracked, with periodic reminders by chat until each is marked done.

## Approach

Nothing is final until a person approves it, and the work does not stop at the document. The minutes exist to get the actions done, so the action items carry on as tracked work after the meeting ends.
