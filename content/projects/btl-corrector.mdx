**BTL Corrector** validates the receipts attached to promotional activity reports. It reads each one and checks it against the rules, so reviewers look at the exceptions instead of every page.

## What it does

- **Wording check**: compares the receipt's wording with what the rules require.
- **Figures and dates**: checks amounts and dates for consistency.
- **Completeness**: looks for the stamp, signature and duty stamp a valid receipt needs.
- **Actionable result**: returns a status with the part that needs to be corrected or reviewed.

## Approach

The rules are explicit and the output points at a location in the document. A reviewer can confirm a flagged problem in seconds, and the submitter knows what to fix without a back-and-forth.
