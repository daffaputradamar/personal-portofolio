A collection tool for media reporting. It watches a defined list of sites and builds the dataset the monthly report is made from.

## What it does

- **Collection**: gathers the link and title of each new article from the listed sources.
- **Tagging**: an LLM reads each article and records the product, brand, company and type it concerns.
- **Fixed scope**: only the sources on the agreed list are monitored.
- **Export**: the data comes out in the shape the reporting needs.

## Approach

Collection and interpretation are kept apart. Scraping produces a plain record of what was published; the model's tags are a layer on top that can be re-run or corrected without collecting everything again.
