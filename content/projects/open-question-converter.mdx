A tool for analysts who work with survey data. It takes the free-text answers to open questions and sorts them into the categories the business has agreed on.

## What it does

- **Classification against a master list**: every answer is assigned to an agreed category.
- **Splitting**: an answer that makes two points is separated, so each point is counted.
- **Suggestions, not silent changes**: answers that fit nothing are marked, with a proposed new category.
- **Approval**: a proposed category only joins the master list after a person approves it.

## Approach

Topic modelling finds the natural groupings in the answers, and a language model maps them onto the agreed list. The master list stays under human control, so the categories used for reporting cannot drift on their own.
