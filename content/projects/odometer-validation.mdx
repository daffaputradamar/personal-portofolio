A validation step for the mileage recorded at each service. Mileage drives service schedules, warranty checks and claims, so a wrong figure has consequences well beyond the one visit.

## What it does

- **Reading**: detects the odometer in the photo and reads the mileage from it.
- **Photo checks**: looks for signs that the photo is not a genuine, current picture of the vehicle.
- **History checks**: compares the reading with the vehicle's earlier records and its type.
- **Flagging**: mismatches are raised for follow-up instead of being accepted silently.

## Approach

A vision model does the reading and a scheduled job does the comparison against history. Keeping inference as its own service means the model can be improved or replaced without touching the jobs that depend on its output.
