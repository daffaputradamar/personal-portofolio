**Rewangit** generates documents that follow the company's standard, taking a meeting transcript as its raw material, either produced by [Tuturaksara](/projects/tuturaksara) or supplied directly by the user.

## What it does

- **Transcript to document**: takes an unstructured conversation and produces a document in the required internal format.
- **Multiple sources**: accepts output from the transcription pipeline, or a transcript the user already holds.
- **House style enforced**: structure, headings and mandatory sections come from the company template rather than from whatever the model produces unprompted.

## Approach

The generation step is deliberately constrained. Rather than asking a model to "write the document", the pipeline extracts the required fields from the transcript first, then renders them into the template. That separation means an incomplete transcript produces a document with visible gaps instead of plausible-sounding invention, which matters when the output becomes an internal record.
