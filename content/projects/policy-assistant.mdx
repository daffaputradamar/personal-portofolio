A policy assistant connected to the company's HR application. Employees ask about a policy in plain language; the assistant answers from the documents that apply to them.

## What it does

- **Access-aware retrieval**: document collections are visible by grade, by department, publicly or privately, and the assistant only retrieves from what the asker may see.
- **Versions and revisions**: each policy keeps its history instead of being overwritten.
- **What changed**: an AI summary compares two versions of a policy and states the differences.
- **Directory sign-in**: users authenticate with the company directory, with an approval step before access is granted.
- **Administration**: company records, users and an activity log are managed inside the tool.

## Approach

Access control is applied at retrieval, not after generation. A passage the asker is not entitled to never reaches the model, so it cannot leak into an answer.
